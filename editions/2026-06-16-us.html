New · PolicyBench

Introducing PolicyBench: how accurately can AI compute taxes and benefits?

We tested 13 frontier language models on 100 real US households—no tools, just the model reasoning from the prompt—and scored them against PolicyEngine. GPT-5.5 leads, getting 82.6% of outputs within 1% of PolicyEngine’s value, but no model is close to reliable. The clearest pattern is taxes versus benefits: across models, 93.7% of benefit outputs land within 1% versus 76.8% of taxes, with multi-step income tax the hardest at around 52%. We also hand-audited all 3,300 wrong cells—every one was a model error, none a PolicyEngine reference bug.
