Introducing PolicyBench: how accurately can AI compute taxes and benefits?

We tested 13 frontier language models on 100 representative US households and scored them against PolicyEngine. GPT-5.5 leads, getting 80.3% of its tax and benefit figures to the dollar. The leaderboard also lets you slice accuracy by program—multi-step calculations like income tax are the hardest.
